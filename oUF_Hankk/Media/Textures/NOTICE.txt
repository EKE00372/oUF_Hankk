Resources/combo.tga, Resources/holypower.tga, Resources/soulshards.tga and Resources/totems.tga
are high-resolution redraws based on that author-version backup's resource
shapes: round pip, horned holy-power cross diagonal soul shard and broad totem pillar.
Each 512x128 atlas has 128x128 base, fill and tintable outer-glow cells,
followed by an unused transparent cell. The new curves, antialiasing,
12px source outline, soft shadow and glow replace the low-resolution BLPs;
the shard also has broad, high-contrast grayscale facets and a bright ridge.
Its fill coverage, base and glow are unchanged by the facet revision.
Lua supplies resource colors.

statusicons.tga is a high-resolution, tintable reconstruction of the shapes in statusicons.blp
from the same author-version backup, oUF_Hank_v3/textures/statusicons.blp.
The resting icon uses three clean geometric Zs, individually rotated toward
the original directions, instead of tracing low-resolution raster edges.
The crown has four tips and a smooth curved base. Combat, PvP and assistant
keep the v6 hand-authored bodies. All use 4x sampling. The atlas is 1024x128, with
eight 128px cells. Display geometry stays at 24-unit slots, plus 4 units of
padding on each side. An 8px source rim is 2 UI units at the default size.
White bodies and diffuse white halos take the same Lua tint; the black rim
and facial details remain black. Near/far halos use sigma 4/10 and opacity
0.42/0.52, with 5px far spread and an 8px smooth edge fade. Cell boundaries
and unused cells are transparent. Previous materials and recipes remain
in Artwork/Tools/texture/variants; parameters and tests are in SOURCES.md.
The same attribution and unconfirmed distribution rights above apply.

The border/shadow helpers in API.lua and the castbar styling/native-binding
approach in Core/Castbar.lua are adapted from oUF_Ruri at the same revision.
The power palette and named/numeric color setup in Tags.lua are also adapted
from oUF_Ruri's Tags.lua at the same revision:
https://github.com/EKE00372/oUF_Ruri/blob/b25ce092c3b52c019459596549cc3a1616ddd58f/Tags.lua
Hankk uses its own layout, font, castbar colors and remaining/total time format.
Core/Auras.lua adapts Ruri's aura border/shadow appearance, native duration
formatter and unit-relation update pattern from Core/Auras.lua at the same
revision. Hankk uses its own font, 20-unit icons, target/focus containers,
group limits and focus filters; no Ruri spell lists are included.
