Resources/combo.tga, Resources/holypower.tga and Resources/soulshards.tga
are high-resolution redraws based on that author-version backup's resource
shapes: round pip, horned holy-power cross and diagonal soul shard.
Each 512x128 atlas has 128x128 base, fill and tintable outer-glow cells,
followed by an unused transparent cell. The new curves, antialiasing,
12px source outline, soft shadow and glow replace the low-resolution BLPs;
the shard also has new grayscale facets. Lua supplies resource colors.

sources and the retired runtime version remain in Artwork, outside the
addon package. Build parameters, output hashes and attribution are in
Artwork/Tools/texture/resources.json and SOURCES.md in the development repo.

statusicons.tga is a brightened, tintable derivative of statusicons.blp
from the same author-version backup, oUF_Hank_v3/textures/statusicons.blp.
Hank's combat, resting, leader, PvP and assistant bodies keep their original
24px size, now padded by 4px on each side in 32px cells. Their grey artwork is remapped to white;
the colored body keeps its original alpha. Only the outer black edge is
gently trimmed with a 30% blend toward a one-pixel inset; inner black details
are protected. A black soft shadow (sigma 1.4px, opacity 0.65) extends into
the padding without changing colored pixels or the solid inner rim. Cell
edges and unused cells are transparent. Lua supplies colors.
The same attribution and unconfirmed distribution rights above apply.

The border/shadow helpers in API.lua and the castbar styling/native-binding
approach in Core/Castbar.lua are adapted from oUF_Ruri at the same revision.
The power palette and named/numeric color setup in Tags.lua are also adapted
from oUF_Ruri's Tags.lua at the same revision:
https://github.com/EKE00372/oUF_Ruri/blob/b25ce092c3b52c019459596549cc3a1616ddd58f/Tags.lua
Hankk uses its own layout, font, castbar colors and remaining/total time format.
Core/Auras.lua adapts Ruri's aura border/shadow appearance, native duration
formatter and unit-relation update pattern from Core/Auras.lua at the same
revision. Hankk uses its own font, 20-unit icons, target/focus containers,
group limits and focus filters; no Ruri spell lists are included.
